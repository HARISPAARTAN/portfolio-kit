portfolio-kit

The shared tokens, components and browser helpers of the portfolio demos, in both themes. Every number on this page is invented.

Colour tokens

The values of the current theme with their contrast ratios, computed on this page with the formula of tools/contrast.mjs.

Typography

Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers. Scale: 12, 14, 16, 20, 28 and 40 px.

KPI tiles

Rendered with renderKpi from the orders below, so they follow the filters. Direction shows as ▲ ▼ ● as well as in colour.

Table, filters and CSV

Forty invented orders. Sort any column, page through them and filter by region or status. The filters live in a filter bus and are saved with createStore; the CSV button exports the rows in their current order.

Pills, buttons, inputs and tabs

Pills tint their colour into the surface and mix a quarter of the text colour into their label, so every pill reads at 4.5:1 or more.

Chart palette

The categorical palette and the two ramps of echarts-theme.js for the current theme, checked with an OKLCH palette validator for lightness, chroma, colour-vision separation and contrast. ECharts itself is not loaded here.
